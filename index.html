How To Play

Find the hidden word in 6 tries.

Every guess has to be a real five-letter word. Press the enter key to submit it.

Once you submit a guess, the tiles change color to show how close you were.

Examples

The letter W is in the word, and in the right spot.

The letter I is in the word, but in a different spot.

The letter U is not in the word anywhere.

A new puzzle is released daily at midnight.
